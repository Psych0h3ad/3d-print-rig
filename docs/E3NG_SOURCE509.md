# E3NG

View the E3NG v1.2 assembly with an Orbiter 2 extruder and Revo Voron hotend.
Switch between the printer and its toolhead, change printed-part colors, and
save a configuration. **Source colors** restores the original appearance.

The model is assembled from RH3D's publicly released STL files and separately
credited public component models. It does not use the paid E3NG STEP assembly.
An assembled STEP download is not provided. See the
[source notices](../site/licenses/e3ng-source509.txt) and
[component credits](../site/licenses/e3ng-source509-attribution.json).

This is a mechanical reference. The controller board geometry, bed thermistor
and internal electrical wiring are not modeled. Rear latches are shown fitted without simulating
their bending. The bed spacers use Fanta_r's model, shortened for display.
Removed print supports are listed with the relevant parts.

Movement previews do not establish usable travel or clearance throughout a real
printer. Use RH3D's assembly instructions when building the machine.
