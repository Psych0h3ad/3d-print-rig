# Trinity Crossbow source assemblies

[Open the assembled head](https://psych0h3ad.github.io/3d-print-rig/viewer/toolheads.html?configuration=trinity87__v13).

Ten source STEP variants from [Martin-Ivanc](https://github.com/Martin-Ivanc/Trinity-Crossbow-Cutter/tree/231a16281d6d34b1b3b0ee8b65ae0b4f00fde7e7)
retain their native fan, hotend, nozzle, Beacon RevD, carriage and Crossbow
hardware. They cover Monolith / VORON belt paths, 6 / 9–10 mm source versions,
Chube Compact / Dragon ACE Volcano, and the two supplied Chube variants without
a cutter. The source-only reference does not register a printer installation.

Native Annex Sherpa Mini Release 2 Rev1 includes its motor, gears, bearings and
fasteners, with the standard front and short idler. The filament datum and
43.5 mm mounting-hole pitch are aligned to the source seat. Native ISO 7380
M3×16 mounting screws replace undersized source screws or fill missing mounts.
The Monolith Chube 9–10 mm cutter/guide subassembly is translated rigidly by
0.25 mm along its native depth axis to align its measured mounting holes with
the Trinity body and Sherpa seat. Original solids are preserved. The original
offset caused two screw/body intersections; native intersection checks are
rerun on the corrected placement.
Full assemblies and belt-clip test parts also appear in Component CAD.

Construction logo negatives are omitted. Six Chube ducts require trim/topology
repair: bounds remain unchanged and each volume delta is below 0.02 mm³.
Source and resulting asset hashes, transforms and repair records accompany the
models. There is no mesh decimation or generic replacement hardware.

Native extruder mating review, exported-mesh/material checks and browser review
are separate evidence. Complete machine travel, Beacon keepout and sensing,
wiring, tolerances and continuous cutter actuation are not certified. Belt
width labels describe the author's source variants, not a new physical-fit
certificate. Missing variants are not fabricated.
