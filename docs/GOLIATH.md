# Goliath reference CAD and Sphinx assemblies

Design: VzBoT team. Manufacturer: Mellow3D. Geometry comes directly from [VzBoT3D/Goliath](https://github.com/VzBoT3D/Goliath/tree/f49cafc26169a8e766e50aee41ebe81e91cb10e5), revision `f49cafc26169a8e766e50aee41ebe81e91cb10e5`, under **CC BY-NC 4.0**. The original license and attribution are retained in `site/licenses/goliath/`. Sphinx, Sherpa, fan and sensor terms remain separate.

The component viewer contains the separate upstream Air and Water mockups, including Water fittings, and the original **Short + water-cooled Vz-HextrudORT assembly**. Short is not a scaled long hotend or a generic air/water option. Its two invalid original solids are retained as reference surfaces; they are not certified analytic solids. Its coolant inspection volume is omitted, while physical tubes, heatbreak and extruder remain. Upstream Air/Water hotend bodies have no internal part breakdown, so internal hardware and material boundaries are not invented.

Twenty-four Sphinx tLW combinations use the author's dedicated Goliath prints: VORON or Monolith, Air or Water, Sherpa Mini R2 standard front with short or long idler, and no probe, Cartographer V4 Standard or Beacon RevH Normal. Air includes the native rear 2510 fan. Water retains its original fittings and omits the air fan; no water circuit or hoses are added. Original dimensions are retained using rigid placement transforms.

Mounting checks use the native four hotend axes, mating face, MGN12H plane and 20 × 20 mm carriage axes. Four quarter-turn orientations were examined: Air 0°/90° clear the head; the selected 0° orientation also fits Water without moving native fittings. Other orientations intersect prints, the fan or clamps. Static checks retain and disclose the original approximately 0.0195 mm³ Sherpa motor/bracket contact. New hotend bodies do not intersect the prints, extruder or cold-side cooling components in the selected pose.

At nozzle contact, Cartographer coil gaps are approximately **2.625 mm for Air** and **2.875 mm for Water**; Beacon gaps are **2.600 mm** and **2.850 mm**. Physical probe bodies remain above that plane. Metal parts are checked against a conservative box enclosing each manufacturer's metal keepout. These are static checks; full printer travel, mounting fasteners, cables, probe operation, airflow and thermal/water circuit behavior are not certified. VORON variants use existing 6 mm machine registration; the new Monolith registration is limited to 6 mm. No SIBOOR 9 mm AWD fit is inferred.

Native reference modules, measured placements, source pins, file hashes and static checks are supplied in the separate `viewer-v44/viewer-goliath-sources.zip` release asset. Existing CHC XL mounting references and affiliate links remain distinct from these Goliath hotends. Standard assembled printer STEP downloads are unchanged.

The existing Sphinx Beacon/Cartographer reference screws are also rotated to put their heads against the PCB underside. Their native dimensions, axes and exterior envelopes remain unchanged. The corrected screws are rechecked against the original continuous machine travel domains and all original Sphinx print bodies. Existing positive head witnesses involve unchanged parts and remain available.
