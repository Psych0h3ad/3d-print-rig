# DoomCube 2

[Open the 350 mm reference](https://psych0h3ad.github.io/3d-print-rig/viewer/community.html?machine=doomcube2_350_reference).
Choose DoomCube 2 / FrankenVoron / 350 in the printer selector. The Frame option
switches the original square 4040 and rounded 404020 frames with nested 2020
extrusions. Printed base/accent colors, frame color and image export use the
same controls as the other community printers.

The model uses the original stock MGN9 / 6 mm gantry and Afterburner/Mosquito
assembly from the author's 350 mm CAD v12. Alternative rails and probes that
conflict with that assembly are hidden. Other sizes, gantries and toolheads are
not registered. Axis controls are disabled for this static reference.

The original [FrankenVoron source](https://github.com/FrankenVoron/DoomCube-2/tree/02baa8c02c8984d46450843e8b41f24b135f6478)
is pinned to that revision. Its GPL-3.0 license and embedded component credits
are retained. This display does not add or reshape hardware. Native printed
source solids 656, 675 and 679 are invalid; their finite meshes are retained,
and mounting/physical clearance remains unverified. Built-in supports have not
been positively identified or removed. The anonymous jog knob and chain-end
connector retain their source colors because their manufacturing identity is
unresolved.

[Appearance evidence](DOOMCUBE_APPEARANCE_103.json) records the exact source
identities, printed face partitions and purchased materials.
[Current release coverage](MACHINE_REVIEW_COVERAGE.json) separates automated
asset checks from native mounting and browser evidence.
