# ELEGOO Neptune references

Neptune 3, Neptune 3 Pro and Neptune 3 Plus use the complete assemblies from
ELEGOO's public 3DXML files. Open them from Machines or choose ELEGOO in the
printer selector. They can also be placed in the room planner.

These are static CAD references. Axis movement, replacement toolheads and
printed-part color controls are unavailable. Source part colors and positions
are retained; bitmap decals and CAD transparency are omitted. Image export and
camera save/load are available. Build sizes are product specifications from
the official manuals, not measured travel limits or clearance certificates.
