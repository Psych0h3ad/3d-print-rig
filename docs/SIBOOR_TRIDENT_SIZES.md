# SIBOOR Trident sizes

The printer selector registers the JUNE CNC AWD kit at 300 and 350 mm. The 300 mm viewer uses the acquired native 300 mm assembly, including its 380 mm carbon beam, 350 mm rails, 300 mm bed, enclosure and ClickyClacky door. Rigid hardware retains its native dimensions. This is not a uniformly scaled 350 mm model.

The sized loader pins every manifest, model, configuration, color and endstop asset by checksum. Unknown sizes, missing files and wrong machine identities fail loading. Stock recipes use 9 mm AWD belts; the R2 swap uses its native 300 mm gantry and 6 mm belt route. Endstop mesh identities and hinge positions follow the selected size. The existing standard 300 mm STEP download remains linked to the native default assembly.

The support index composes the same sized recipes and head registration as the viewer. It does not register frame docks or Monolith mounting for the 300 mm kit without corresponding mounting evidence.

Eight surplus rail screw occurrences are omitted while retaining all 48 native fixing positions. Printed and hardware materials remain separate; side, top and door sheets remain transparent, with the rear and floor opaque. Source identity and documentation versions appear in the public source catalog.

See [size review](SIBOOR_TRIDENT_SIZES_QA.json) for current model and adapter hashes, native solid inspection, actual loaded catalog checks, sampled motion and browser evidence. Rail screw mating axes and body clearance are checked against native solids. Native bearing inserts have a small nonzero rail intersection and are not certified. Outside the registered chain routing envelope, the viewer explicitly labels its reference-shape fallback. Full physical fit of all hotend, probe, dock and wiring combinations is not certified.
