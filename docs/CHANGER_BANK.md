# StealthChanger bank

V2.4 machine pages offer a frame-mounted ModularDock bank and a single active StealthChanger V1.1 head. Choose one to three tools on 250 mm V2.4 references, or one to four on the registered 300/350 mm V2.4 references. Individual slots accept Xol / Sherpa Mini / Rapido 2 UHF or JabberWocky / Conch. Repeated heads and mixed banks are supported. Tool selection moves only the active head with the gantry; other heads and all docks stay attached to the fixed frame, including during V2.4 Z motion. The standalone workbench also offers these source bank layouts. [INDX tools](INDX.md) use a separate bank with one Smart Head.

Save/load configuration JSON includes `tool_bank`. Shared URLs include `tools`, containing the enabled state, active index and source configuration for each slot. Unknown, off-machine, over-capacity and invalid active-index states are rejected. Changing to an unregistered parked-head combination disables the bank and retains the normal single-head viewer. The main head selector still offers the other registered hotend/extruder combinations, including SB; the mount selector is visible outside advanced settings.

`TOOLCHANGER_BANK.json` supplies the parked transforms, 90 mm pitch, conservative side reserve and machine-specific top-extrusion datum. The original top bracket mating plane registers to the underside of the actual frame beam, with its two M5 axes aligned to the bottom slot. Source-native VORON M5 T-nuts use the measured slot seating position, 0.35 mm deeper than the initial flipped source pose. Their geometry and dimensions are unchanged. Bottom-crossbar clamp screws/nuts are omitted for the top-mount preview. No print supports were removed from these selected dock bodies.

Native static checks cover Xol and JabberWocky head bodies against their printed docks, top bracket and fixing bolts against all nine registered frame profiles, and T-nuts against those slots. The top bracket has a sub-0.001 mm³ mating-face residual. Individual source head warnings remain visible. This is a CAD arrangement preview: retaining hardware, stabilizing L brackets/links, cable paths, door fit, travel sweeps, automatic docking paths and mechanical stability are not certified. No tool-changing G-code or physical control is implemented.

SB is deliberately excluded from parked-bank choices: the supplied standard SB rear stop intersects the V1.1 backplate, and its UHF shroud also intersects the base. Lowering the stop was rejected because its screw axes no longer fit the source slots. Source printed geometry is retained; incompatible shapes are not silently cut or replaced. SB remains available as one installed StealthChanger head with its existing native-fit warnings.

Original models come from DraftShift/StealthChanger at `50e3c769297b273ac390fb33b455aa7f4dfe3099` and DraftShift/ModularDock at `597fad34f3a1233ed1c182ec221a8da9ba68ac33` (GPL-3.0). The added T-nuts come from VoronDesign/Voron-Trident at `a8628f48546948ce1fc15511b7765b7f31f80722` (GPL-3.0). Original toolhead, hotend and extruder licenses remain separate. See [third-party notices](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), existing source bundles, and the `viewer-v20` bank source archive for editable native geometry and source pins.

The standard bank is unavailable on Trident. Native body checks at the actual nozzle plane reproduce the bed/dock collision. Lowering the bed to clear the dock would prevent printing and is not a valid solution. Saved states and URLs cannot re-enable this incompatible bank. Individual installed-head references remain available. Bed reference motion is bounded by the native Z carriage/rail envelope; a nozzle above that limit is reported as unreachable.

The source [Parallel Dock for Trident](https://github.com/angelassie/Parallel-Dock-for-Trident-Stealthchanger/tree/d3b4818d34c3b130b33dee4b4e4bbdf8b5f0e64d) uses dedicated backplates/cowls and horizontal changes. It has not been registered to these source machine/head assemblies. A compatible complete Trident bank is still required; standard docks are not substituted or silently reshaped.
