# Trident source assembly references

The interactive source view for the stock 250 mm Trident includes separate
panel, clip, magnet, hinge, handle, rear-panel and filament-path references.
Each operation links to the pinned official manual and identifies the original
source occurrences used there. Preparation displays keep complete source groups
apart until their installation operation; complete-assembly comparison and
leaving the view restore their original source placement.

Use **Dim earlier steps** to distinguish current or reused source items from
earlier work. Stage checks and individual source-part checks remain independent.
The source model provides identities and locations, not a purchasing BOM or a
fastening sequence inferred from part names.

Progress from the previous 12-chapter 250 mm guide keeps source-part checks and
unchanged chapter checks. Wiring, panels and filament-path work require review
after their operations are separated. Older progress and files remain intact;
the viewer rejects a record from another source or manual revision. A rejected
stage change does not accept the requested progress file. Fixing the failed
load and retrying is separate from marking an operation complete.

The 300 and 350 mm source views retain their own chapter plans. Printed V2.4
views retain their source-specific plans, and CNC versions provide part maps
without borrowing the printed assembly sequence.

The manual is [VoronDesign's Trident assembly manual at the pinned source
revision](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/VoronDesign/Voron-Trident/a8628f48546948ce1fc15511b7765b7f31f80722/Manual/Assembly_Manual_Trident.pdf).
Original CAD/source attribution and GPL-3.0 terms remain attached to the model.
The editable reference descriptors are tracked separately from their exact
compressed delivery. Both encoded and decoded identities are checked before
creating the guide; missing or changed reference data cannot install a different
source plan.

These references do not complete the entire manual or certify insertion paths,
threads, torque, retention or physical fit. Optional frame foam, the rear-panel
fastener specification difference and source mounting findings retain their
explicit limits. A source part at its authored pose is not clearance evidence.
