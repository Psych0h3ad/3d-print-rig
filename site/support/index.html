How to read this index

Generated at publication from the viewer catalogs and combination rules. Counts represent registered configurations, not multiplied component totals.

Counts exclude colors, poses, independent add-ons and dock-bank arrangements. V0 counts are Mod selections that satisfy mounting rules.

Standalone-only parts still need machine mounts or assembly data registered. Unregistered does not mean physically incompatible.
