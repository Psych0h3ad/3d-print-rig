Rapido X.STEP provides the hotend and a separate GrooveMount adapter.
Rapido X-voron.3mf provides the dedicated Stealthburner front/rear mounts.
The source repository contains no explicit CAD redistribution license.
Public availability does not imply GPL, Creative Commons or unrestricted
commercial use. All rights remain with the original authors.

The viewer preserves native dimensions and uses rigid placement. The hotend
body has restored native face trims; its volume changes by less than 0.06 mm3
and its envelope by less than 0.001 mm. Original and prepared CAD are separate.
Four-bolt installations omit the separate GrooveMount adapter. The front
mount remains the original non-watertight triangle mesh. Analytic STEP exports
exclude the two mesh-only mounts; matching original/placed STL is supplied.
Slicer project settings are not part of the geometry distribution.

These terms apply to their respective parts; they do not relicense Phaetus CAD.
