Upstream credits retained: DraftShift Design (StealthChanger), hartk1213
(Anthead and umbilical styling), Armchair Heavy Industries (A4T).
The original GPL notices for Anthead and StealthChanger and the A4T
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 notice accompany these references. These are separate
component terms; this notice does not relicense the underlying A4T design.

Display assemblies retain original placed bodies, hardware and dimensions:
left/right stock-idler docks with dedicated Anthead or A4T blockers;
LDO/FYSETC-specific shovers; Anthead SF ducts and catch screws;
A4T Low Rider backplate and hardware. Alternative blockers and BFI
idler inserts are not combined with the stock-idler dock.

The Shover print support bodies (source keys 10 and 22, five solids each)
are omitted. No other solid is removed to mask an interference. The stock
idler insert is retained: it is part of the dock body, not a print support.

These are individual author component assemblies, not assembled printers
or complete toolheads. Printer attachment, passive lift/docking, bed
clearance and continuous exchange motion are not certified. A preliminary
stock-idler placement on the current Trident R2 CAD intersects the idler;
no machine installation or parked tool bank is enabled. Existing Trident
StealthChanger bank restrictions are retained. Original screws and nuts
remain at their source assembly placements, not relocated by bounding boxes.

Preferred author CAD, placed native bodies, source checksums and acquisition
records accompany the source archive. Author macros remain documentation;
the viewer neither executes them nor presents a runnable exchange sequence.
