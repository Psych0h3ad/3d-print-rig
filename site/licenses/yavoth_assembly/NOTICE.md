# Yavoth native assembly previews

Yavoth v1 and Alternative Voron Mounts: chirpy2605/voron,
cc2e749d09d7f24418f9f8c73c90bcefb1e29584, GPL-3.0.
https://github.com/chirpy2605/voron/tree/cc2e749d09d7f24418f9f8c73c90bcefb1e29584

Native rigid placements combine the original standard or 5 mm rear-offset
cowl, LED holder/insert, two angled 4010 blowers, one front-intake 2510 fan,
Sherpa Mini Release 2 Rev1 or Orbiter V2, and distinct Rapido v1 HF/UHF
hardware. The original Alternative Voron Mounts Lite carriage has separate
6 and 9 mm versions. Only the three upper attachment points are referenced.
Lower support/probe carrier, external mounting fasteners, wiring, toolhead
board, rail block and belt tails are not included in the standalone assembly.
The machine supplies its existing rail block. No toolchanger fit is implied.

The 6 mm carriage's separate manufacturing support, native key 26, is omitted.
No cowl/carriage body material is removed. Native dimensions are retained.
Upper lateral axes differ by 0.04025 mm; the central axis differs by
0.17077 mm. Native cowl/carriage contacts are retained: 3.078 mm3 for
6 mm and 110.456 mm3 for 9 mm. Standard Sherpa configurations retain
rear-housing/carriage intersections of 1.590 mm3 (6 mm) or 3.372 mm3
(9 mm); rear-offset cowls remove those extruder-body intersections.
All these contacts are displayed as comparison conditions. They do not
certify physical fit, tolerances, belt retention, homing or continuous travel.
The UHF fan/duct assembly extends 8.5 mm lower. No Rapido v2 hardware
is substituted. Print-only older references remain explicitly separate.
