# FYSETC CNC Bed Fans & Connection Mount

Author: driftrotor (@driftrotor_763225).

Files dated 2024-05-01; page updated 2024-05-02. Retrieved 2026-10-04.
License shown on the source page: GNU GPL v3.0.

The two original 3MF files are included in the corresponding source archive.
Each contains two source objects. The fan bracket is identical in both
files and is displayed once. The original and mirrored connection mounts
are separate alternatives. All three meshes retain their source dimensions
and complete ModelPart volumes; no support geometry was removed.
Print-bed layout transforms are not assembly transforms. The three parts
are displayed individually, not as a registered printer assembly.

The author's instructions specify up to two 3010 axial fans, 2–4 M3x14
BHCS fan screws, 6–10 M3 heat inserts and two M3x20 mounting screws.
Fans, PCB and fasteners are not part of these source meshes and are not
invented or claimed installed in this component view.

The STEP has one valid CNC bed solid. Its two side mounting holes match
the 58 mm pitch measured in the original fan and connection mounts.
This confirms hole pitch only, not mounting seats, PCB clearance or full
Z travel. The bed repository contains no license declaration. The bed
STEP and derived bed geometry are not redistributed here; the Printables
mount license is not extended to that separate GitHub source.
