Changes: Native CAD surface tessellation, checked small-hardware mesh reduction,
material roles and mounted axis groups. Original placements and dimensions remain
unchanged. The standard view uses the 200 mm carbon bed, Orbiter V2.0, Dragon HF with its native extension adapter and matching nozzle,
two 5015 part-cooling fans and a 4010 hotend fan. It is the author's complete
printer assembly; the separately published electronics box is not included.
The 40 EXTRAS leaves (assembly guides, tools and optional frame accessories) and
the short, unextended nozzle are optional reference geometry. The extended
nozzle protrudes below the native long silicone sock; no hardware is repositioned. No identified embedded
printing supports were removed from the native assembly. Source cable/PCB leaves
333 and 859 contain invalid source BREP geometry; their display surfaces are retained.
X/Z belts are 6 mm; the Y belt is 9 mm. Preview controls are conservative offsets
from the native CAD pose, not firmware home coordinates. The reverse Bowden uses the author's toolhead guide's 600 mm service length,
native fitting centres and normals, and a display route with 25 mm bend radii.
The route stays above the crossbar during travel. The lower Z preview limit
keeps the nozzle and auxiliary fan flaps above the native bed surface.
Flexible source belts and cables follow their endpoints for display; tension, link mechanics and physical
cable bending are not simulated. New toolhead or mod combinations are not implied.
