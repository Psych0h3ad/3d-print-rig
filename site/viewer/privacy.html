Your choice

Analytics is optional and based on your consent. The Google tag is not loaded and no analytics request is sent before you choose Allow statistics. Declining does not limit the viewer. Open Usage statistics to change or withdraw your choice at any time; withdrawal stops future measurement and removes this project's analytics cookies. It does not undo processing that took place before withdrawal.

Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals disable measurement. Embedded viewers, local previews and explicitly excluded review visits are not measured.

What analytics receives

After opt-in, Google Analytics 4 receives viewer page paths, a fixed page title, cookie-based identifiers and technical information such as browser, device and language. Network requests also disclose an IP address to Google, which can derive approximate location. Query strings, fragments, referrer details, saved configurations and imported model files are not included in our analytics events.

We use these measurements to understand usage and improve the viewer. Our integration disables Google Signals and advertising personalization. We do not use these measurements to sell personal information, target advertisements or make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Public statistics

A public report, when available, shows aggregated page views, users, daily trends and countries. We do not publish IP addresses, cookie identifiers or individual browsing records. Country estimates may be inaccurate, especially with VPNs. Refusals, blockers and reporting delays mean the totals are not an exact count of all visits.

Storage and retention

The analytics cookies _ga and _ga_SEV0T0PFTB identify a browser and its session within this project. Their expiry is limited to 180 days without automatic renewal. Your versioned allow or decline preference is stored locally for 180 days. Essential preferences, such as language, display settings and saved configurations, are separate from analytics and are not sent to Google by this integration.

The GA4 property uses a two-month user and event data retention setting, without resetting user retention on new activity. Eligible data is removed in Google's monthly deletion process. Standard aggregate reports are not governed by that setting and may remain for the project's statistical history. Contact us about deletion requests and available information; do not send passwords or sensitive documents.

Providers and international processing

Google LLC in the United States provides analytics, and GitHub provides site and model delivery. These services receive network information needed to respond to requests. After analytics consent, data is transmitted online to Google and may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to your local data protection authority. This includes relevant rights under European and UK data protection law, Japan's APPI and applicable US state laws. We do not discriminate against visitors who decline analytics. We may need proportionate information to locate data or verify a request, and will explain if we cannot associate an aggregate with you.

If you are below the age at which you may consent where you live, leave analytics disabled unless your parent or guardian authorizes it.

Other websites and changes

Sponsor, affiliate and source links lead to other websites with their own privacy practices. Public reports also have their provider's policies. Material changes to our analytics purposes or providers require a new choice before measurement resumes; the updated date and consent version identify the policy you accepted.
