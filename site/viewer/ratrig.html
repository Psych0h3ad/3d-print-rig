Cables use a continuous motion preview. Slack and hanging routes are proposals; physical length, contact and stiffness remain unverified.

Save configuration

Save pose, IDEX mode, colors, visibility, lighting and camera. This differs from G-code SAVE/RESTORE.

G-code trace

Coordinate/lighting preview of expanded commands. Apply supported commands line by line and stop at an unsupported line.

Applying a mode, resetting the CAD pose or loading a configuration initializes G-code state.

Supports G21, G90/G91, G0/G1 XYZ/F, G92 XYZ, ACTIVATE_EXTRUDER, SET_DUAL_CARRIAGE, SET_LED and SAVE/RESTORE_GCODE_STATE. Extrusion, temperatures, homing, probing, independent Z tilt, Jinja/full RatOS/Klipper macros and physical hardware execution are unsupported.

ACTIVATE_EXTRUDER selects a hotend; SET_DUAL_CARRIAGE selects the motion carriage. G-code restore does not restore lighting, colors, the active hotend, IDEX mode or unselected heads.
