Model scope
  • Original assembly with TZ-V6 3.0, a 3010 hotend fan, two 4010 blowers, BMG gears and NEMA14 motor.
  • EBB36/42, pin/bolt pivots and the two Y-block variants are switched independently.
  • One original EBB42 board component fails solid validation. Its original geometry is retained for display.
  • The dock beam is 330 mm. The original bracket outer width is 407.2 mm; the author specifies 407 mm for assembly. Dimensions are unchanged.
  • The original CAD also includes spare parts. Bed/frame clearance, travel limits and toolchange G-code are unverified.
  • This is not presented as a model with embedded print supports removed.
